Our Story

Bringing Astrology Into A Digital World.

Astrology has a long-standing place in India's cultural and intellectual traditions. BABA ASTRO is being developed to bring selected astrology-related resources and digital consultation options together in one accessible place.

Our planned service experience includes astrologer chat, voice and video consultation, Kundli resources, AI-assisted astrology, Panchang and Muhurat information, and daily horoscope content.

We aim to serve a diverse audience, including people living outside India who want convenient access to astrology-related information and services.
